Quick Summary

Meridian v1 (the paper-trading demo) is done and unchanged. Phase 2 proposes three additions: video consultation rooms, instant trade-broadcast notifications, and a real-money funded tier starting at a €250 minimum deposit. None of this is built yet — this document scopes it and collects your answers before we write any code.

READ THIS FIRST
Adding real deposits and trade broadcasting changes what kind of business this is. A demo with fake money is software. A platform that takes real deposits and tells subscribers what to copy is a financial service — and in the EU, copy-trading has a specific regulatory classification (see below) that decides whether this needs a license before launch, not after. This isn't a reason to slow down — it's the reason the build order at the end puts the legal-sensitive piece last.

Phase 2 Additions (Proposed)

Video Call & Communication Suite

Admin-hosted 1:1 consultations and/or group live-trading rooms, plus a direct-message support channel. Built on a hosted video provider (not raw WebRTC). A booking/scheduling flow is a separate piece this needs.

Trade Broadcast Notification System

The instant an admin executes a trade, subscribed users get an alert — in-app and/or push notification.

THE MOST IMPORTANT DECISION IN THIS DOCUMENT
EU regulators have published explicit guidance on this exact feature: if the platform automatically places the trade into a follower's account, that is legally “portfolio management” and requires a MiFID license — regardless of deposit size. If the system only notifies the user and a person manually places their own order, it does not trigger that classification.

Recommendation: build this as notify-only by design. Automatic replication is a separate, later decision requiring its own legal review before any code is written for it.

Funded Tier — €250 Minimum Deposit

A real-money tier alongside the existing free practice tier. Real deposits mean: (1) KYC becomes mandatory, not optional; (2) funds are routed through a licensed broker partner rather than held directly — self-custody needs $250,000+ in licensing, a partner avoids that; (3) since € signals EU/UK users, the broker partner needs EU/UK regulatory coverage, not just US coverage.

Recommended Build Sequence

  1. Accounts & persistence (Supabase) — needed before everything else here.
  2. Real market data (Polygon.io) — replaces the client-side random walk.
  3. Notify-only trade broadcasting — explicitly not auto-replicating trades.
  4. Video consulting (Daily.co) — 1:1 rooms first, group rooms once validated.
  5. Funded tier (€250 minimum) — only after a broker partner with EU/UK coverage is contracted, KYC is implemented, and legal has confirmed Question 9's answer doesn't trigger portfolio-management licensing.

Steps 1–4 are engineering-led. Step 5 is legal-led with engineering support — it should not start until Section 3, Question 9 has a confirmed answer.
