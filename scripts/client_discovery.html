We've built a working demo of a trading app. It's fully practice-only — no real market data, no real accounts, no real money. Everything resets when you refresh the page. Logo Concept Directions

12 directions were sketched, grouped by theme:

  • Compass / star marks — a spiked compass rose or star-burst, sometimes paired with a monogram, an infinity loop, or a dart shape. Abstract, reads as “navigation,” no IP risk.
  • Anchor — a stylized anchor with curved horns. Nautical and premium-looking.
  • Ship's wheel + hat — a steering wheel with a hat resting on top. Leans close to a specific pirate-captain silhouette — a style caution, not a hard trademark issue.
  • Skull & crossbones with a straw hat — this one directly mirrors the Straw Hat Pirates' Jolly Roger from One Piece. Do not use this commercially.
  • Skull & crossbones, no hat — a generic pirate skull mark. No specific IP tie, but a skull is an odd signal of trust for a trading platform.
  • Pulse / sonar orb — concentric rings around a glowing core. Reads as “live data,” fits a trading dashboard well.
  • Chart + starburst — a rising connected-dot line with a star at the peak. Ties directly to trading, not just the nautical theme.
RECOMMENDATION & SOLUTION
Recommendation: shortlist the anchor, the pulse/sonar orb, and the chart+starburst mark. Solution: all three are original, read as “finance + navigation,” and carry zero trademark risk. Drop the skull-and-crossbones-with-hat mark entirely — it's a near-direct copy of a copyrighted character's emblem.
