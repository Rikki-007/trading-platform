What This Is

Meridian is a working demo of a trading app. It looks and feels real — prices move, you can buy and sell, your balance updates — but it's all practice money. Nothing here connects to real markets, real accounts, or real money yet.

What's Built Right Now

3D background

  • A moving 3D scene behind the app — glowing rings and floating particles.
  • It reacts to your mouse and to scrolling, and the colors shift as you scroll down.

Trading dashboard

  • 6 practice stocks with prices that update every couple seconds.
  • $100,000 in practice money to start.
  • A buy/sell screen with Market and Limit order types.
  • A live order book (shows fake buy/sell offers around the current price).
  • A portfolio screen showing your cash, holdings, and gains/losses.
  • A history log of every trade you make.

Look and feel

  • Dark theme with cyan, gold, and red accent colors.
  • Glass-style navigation bar and side menu.
  • Smooth animations throughout (Framer Motion).

Not Built Yet

To be upfront: a scrolling price ticker and a “system status” indicator were mentioned early on but aren't in the app yet. Easy to add later — just flagging it now so nothing is assumed.

  • No real accounts or login — refreshing the page resets everything.
  • No real market data — all prices are made up.
  • No real money, no payments, no ID checks.
  • No saved history — nothing is stored anywhere.

Logo Concept Directions

12 directions were sketched, grouped by theme:

  • Compass / star marks — a spiked compass rose or star-burst, sometimes paired with a monogram, an infinity loop, or a dart shape. Abstract, reads as “navigation,” no IP risk.
  • Anchor — a stylized anchor with curved horns. Nautical and premium-looking.
  • Ship's wheel + hat — a steering wheel with a hat resting on top. Leans close to a specific pirate-captain silhouette — a style caution, not a hard trademark issue.
  • Skull & crossbones with a straw hat — this one directly mirrors the Straw Hat Pirates' Jolly Roger from One Piece. Do not use this commercially.
  • Skull & crossbones, no hat — a generic pirate skull mark. No specific IP tie, but a skull is an odd signal of trust for a trading platform.
  • Pulse / sonar orb — concentric rings around a glowing core. Reads as “live data,” fits a trading dashboard well.
  • Chart + starburst — a rising connected-dot line with a star at the peak. Ties directly to trading, not just the nautical theme.
RECOMMENDATION & SOLUTION
Recommendation: shortlist the anchor, the pulse/sonar orb, and the chart+starburst mark. Solution: all three are original, read as “finance + navigation,” and carry zero trademark risk. Drop the skull-and-crossbones-with-hat mark entirely — it's a near-direct copy of a copyrighted character's emblem.
